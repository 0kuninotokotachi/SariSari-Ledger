# 🏪 SariSari-Ledger

A simple, **offline** ledger app made for sari-sari store owners — track who owes you money and keep your daily sales in order, no internet needed. 📴

## ✨ What it does

### 💸 Utang (Credit) Tracking
Keep track of every customer who buys "on credit."
- 👤 Add a customer in seconds — just a name (and phone number if you want).
- 📈 See each customer's running utang balance at a glance.
- 🔴 🟢 Color-coded list — red means they still owe you, green means they're all settled up.

### 📊 Dashboard Summary
The home screen greets you with the numbers that matter most:
- 💰 Total unpaid utang across all customers.
- 🙋 How many customers currently owe you money, out of your total customer list.

### 🧾 Daily Sales Logging *(coming soon)*
A simple way to log each day's sales so you always know how the store is doing.

## 🌟 Why store owners will like it

- 📴 **Works fully offline** — no signal? No problem. Everything is saved right on your phone.
- 🔤 **Big text, big buttons** — easy to read and tap, no squinting required.
- ₱ **Peso-ready** — all amounts are shown the way you expect, in ₱.
- ⚡ **Fast and light** — runs smoothly even on budget phones.
